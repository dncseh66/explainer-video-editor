# Channel style guide (from reference/ frames)

## 1. Characters
| Part | Rule | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Head | Perfect circle, pure white `#FFFFFF`, thin outline `#2A2522`. Big: head diameter ≈ 40–45% of an adult's full height. No nose, ears, cheeks or shading. | all |
| Eyes | Two small solid black vertical ovals, set a little above the centre and shifted toward where the character looks (¾ view). Closed = two downward arcs. Happy = two upward arcs. | 05, 08, 10 |
| Mouth | Tiny: a short line (neutral), a small arc (smile/frown), or an open white rounded rectangle with one tooth line (shock/worry). | 04, 08, 12, 13 |
| Brows | Only for strong emotion; short thin strokes. | 00 |
| Neck | Short white neck, visible inside collars. | 04, 05 |
| Body (simple) — DEFAULT | Thin black stick limbs (channel decision); small WHITE oval feet; small WHITE round hands (channel decision, never black balls). | 10, 16 |
| Body (close-up) | Hands become white 4-finger cartoon hands with outline; bare skin on the BODY (chest, limbs in crowds) is peach `#F2D2B6` while the head stays white. | 04, 13, 17 |
| Clothes | Flat fill + one darker shade for folds/right side, a few fold lines, outline. Monk robe brown `#A87650` with rope belt, hood down on shoulders, sandals. Prisoners: torn brown tunic with jagged hem. Guard: mustard tunic + teal sleeves/legs + bucket hat. Nurse: light-blue scrubs + name tag. | 05, 10, 08, 17 |
| Traits | Hair, beards, hats and age go ON TOP of the white head (hair shapes hug the circle). | 00 (scientist mustache) |

## 2. Scenes
- Clean, even dark linework (same weight as characters); flat colour fields with a very soft painterly/watercolour wash, no hard shadows.
- Each scene has ONE dominant colour family: warm terracotta dungeon (17), sage-green vaulted hall (05), teal + mustard prison (10), cool blue-grey snow (06), pastel spring field (14), blue-grey stone (15, 18), mint hospital (08).
- Light = a soft radial glow (torch, fire, window) rather than cast shadows.
- Architecture is simple and readable: rounded stone blocks with gaps, wooden tables with grain lines, arches and vaults.
- Depth: big foreground prop or character, mid-ground action, simple background; wide establishing shots for places (06, 16).

## 3. Infographics
- Pale backgrounds (white grid paper, soft pink/cream), objects white with a light-blue offset shadow (07) or flat anatomical colours (09).
- Labels: hand-lettered ALL CAPS, thin leader lines/arrows; coral `#F08A7E` arrows for "before → after".
- Maps: sand land `#E9C48A` on teal sea `#9ED3CC`, labels in hand lettering, small icons on places (11).

## 4. Motion (from the reference channel + ours)
- Stepped 4-pose walk at 8 fps with a bob; arm hits with anticipation and overshoot; blink every 2–4 s; head squash on surprise.
- Constant slow parallax pan (3 layers) on wide shots; whip-slide transitions (~10 frames, ease-out).
- Literal visual gag on the key narration word.
