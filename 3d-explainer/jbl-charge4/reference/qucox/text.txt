JBL
 is an audio brand under Harman Kardon. As an old-brand audio manufacturer in the United States, JBL has a series of processes from the development of raw materials to the design and production of speaker units, to the design and production of speakers. Its audio product service range is very wide, covering professional and civilian levels, and it can be seen in various audio fields.
This time 52audio brought you the 
JBL CHARGE 4
 4th generation wireless bluetooth speaker. This product continues the design of the previous generation in appearance, with a cylindrical shape and the whole is wrapped by woven mesh. The configuration uses 50*90mm track speakers, the maximum output power is increased to 30W; the charging interface is upgraded to Type-C, the battery life reaches 20h, and it can be used as a power bank; it also supports IPX7 waterproofing and multiple series functions.
52audio has teardown the JBL Flip 5, JBL TUNE 225, JBL T280, JBL TUNE120, JBL Free and other TWS true wireless earbuds. Let’s take a look at the internal structure of this speaker product today!

| Summary
The appearance design of JBL CHARGE 4 wireless bluetooth speaker continues the design of the previous generation and inherits the family-style design language. Cylindrical appearance, large-area woven mesh wrapping, horizontal vertical mode, radiation diaphragms on the left and right sides, and JBL iconic LOGO on the diaphragm, the overall look is relatively simple and highly recognizable. There are many color matching, which enrich the personalized choice of this product.
The internal structure is well-made and detailed. The components are connected by sockets, and the joints of the cavities are cushioned and protected by sealing rubber pads. The configuration uses a speaker unit with a maximum output power of 30W, with two low-frequency radiation diaphragm units on the left and right to enhance the low-frequency sense; and is equipped with TI TPA3128D2 Class D amplifier and PCM5121 audio digital processor, as well as two to minimize the signal source The affected RUNIC RS622 low-noise operational amplifier effectively guarantees the sound quality.
The charging interface is replaced from the previous generation of Micro-USB to Type-C, and the battery capacity has been increased to 7500mAh, providing a long-lasting battery life of up to 20h for the speaker; the battery is equipped with a circuit protection board, which is fixed inside the plastic insulation bracket, and the protection board has Circuit protection IC and NMOS tube; and equipped with a USB-A interface, supporting the power bank function, which can be used for emergency charging of mobile devices such as mobile phones and tablets.
The main control chip adopts Qualcomm QCC5125 low-power bluetooth audio SOC, supports bluetooth 5.0, has low power consumption, high-quality wireless audio and other features; MPS MP3431 high-efficiency fully integrated synchronous boost chip, powers the digital amplifier; SILERGY SY7065A synchronization Rectifier boost IC for USB-A port 5V output; TI TPS2546 USB charging port controller for USB-A port output protection; MPS MP2624 power management IC for built-in battery charging management; and Macronix MX25U6435F memory, AWINIC AW9523B LED Driver, MATSUKI ME4435 P-channel logic enhanced power field effect transistor, etc.
